SunSide privacy

How SunSide handles location and saved settings.

Updated October 13, 2026.

Solar calculations run in your browser. You can use preset coordinates or enter coordinates manually. Browser geolocation is requested only when you press “Use my location”; your browser controls permission. SunSide does not send those coordinates to a geocoder or solar calculation server. If you choose to load climate averages, rounded regional coordinates and your network request are sent to NASA POWER. Precise entered coordinates are not included in that request. NASA may process request logs under its own policies. Climate data is cached only in memory for this session.

“Save in this browser” stores your configuration, including coordinates, in local storage on the device. “Delete & reset” removes that saved record. Saved settings are not automatically loaded. No accounts, advertising, analytics or affiliate tracking are installed.

Cloudflare and GitHub provide hosting and may process routine network request information such as IP addresses and browser details under their own policies. External reference links lead to sites with separate policies. This notice will be updated before additional data collection is introduced.
